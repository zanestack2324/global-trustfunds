About Us

Building wealth through intelligent property investment

Since 2012, Global Trustfunds has democratized access to premium real estate — letting everyday investors benefit from the stability and growth of property.

Our Story

A decade of trusted, transparent investing

Global Trustfunds started with a simple observation: the world's most stable asset class — real estate — was locked behind enormous capital requirements. We set out to change that by fractionalizing premium property so anyone could invest meaningfully.

Today, we manage over $285M in assets across 4 continents, backed by a team of acquisition analysts, underwriters, and property managers who source, vet, and operate every asset in our portfolio.
