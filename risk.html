Risk Disclosure

Important information about the risks associated with property investment.

Overview

Investing in real estate involves risk, including the possible loss of your principal investment. The information provided on this website is for general information purposes only and does not constitute financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

Market Risk

Property values can fluctuate based on changes in the economy, interest rates, local market conditions, and other factors. There is no guarantee that a property will appreciate in value, and in some cases property values may decline.

Liquidity Risk

Real estate is inherently less liquid than other investments. While Global Trustfunds offers a secondary market, we cannot guarantee that you will be able to sell your shares promptly or at the desired price, and selling may be subject to a marketplace fee.

Rental and Occupancy Risk

Returns depend on rental income, which may be affected by vacancies, tenant defaults, property damage, and changes in operating costs. There is no guarantee of a specific return or that rental income will be generated.

Project and Management Risk

The success of a project depends on the performance of property managers, developers, and other third parties. Delays, cost overruns, or mismanagement could reduce returns or result in loss.

Tax Risk

Investment returns may have tax implications. You should consult with a qualified tax advisor regarding your specific circumstances before investing.

No Guarantee

Target returns, APY figures, and projected yields are estimates for illustration only and are not guarantees of future performance. Past performance is not indicative of future results. You should only invest capital you can afford to lose.

By investing through Global Trustfunds, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these risks.
