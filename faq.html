FAQ

Got questions? We have answers

Fractional investing lets you own a small share of a large property. Instead of buying a whole building, you invest as little as $100 in a professionally managed property and earn a proportional share of the rental income and appreciation.

Our properties target annual returns between 9% and 14%, with rental income distributed monthly. Returns depend on the specific property and market conditions.

You can start investing with a minimum of $100. This low entry point makes premium real estate accessible to everyone regardless of budget.

Your shares are liquid on our secondary marketplace, where you can sell them to other investors at any time. Some properties also have a defined hold period, after which you can exit at face value.

We apply rigorous underwriting to every property, diversify across asset classes and regions, and protect your assets with bank grade security and regulated custody. While all investments carry risk, our structured approach minimizes it.
