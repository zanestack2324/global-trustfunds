Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, contact details, and any other details you submit through forms or your account. We also automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, process investments, respond to inquiries, send relevant communications and updates, enhance security, and comply with legal obligations. We may send you marketing communications only with your consent.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, analyze usage, and remember preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Blockchain and Digital Assets

Investments made through Global Trustfunds may be recorded on a blockchain. Wallet addresses and transaction hashes are treated as pseudonymous identifiers and are not linked to your personal identity unless you choose to connect a wallet during a transaction. We use blockchain technology to provide transparent, tamper-evident records of your investments.

Connect your Wallet

To invest with digital assets, we may ask you to connect a self-custody wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect. We never store or have access to your private keys. Transactions are confirmed on chain and broadcast through your own wallet.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform (such as payment processors and hosting providers), and where required by law.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@globaltrustfunds.com.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our contact page.

Risk Disclosure

The risk of loss in trading stocks, options, futures, currencies, foreign equities, and fixed income can be substantial. Before you trade, please read the relevant risk disclosure statements on our Warnings and Disclosures page.

Trading on margin is only for experienced investors with a high risk tolerance. You may lose more than your initial investment. For additional information about margin loan rates, please see our Margin Loan Rates.

Security futures involve a high degree of risk and are not suitable for all investors. The amount you may lose may be greater than your initial investment. Before trading security futures, please read the Security Futures Risk Disclosure Statement.

Structured products and fixed income products such as bonds are complex products that carry additional risk and are not suitable for all investors. Before trading, please read the Risk Warning and Disclosure Statement.
